DEPARTMENT OF LABOR AND EMPLOYMENT

GOVERNMENT INTERNSHIP PROGRAM

(DOLE-GIP)

INTERNSHIP AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made by and between the Department of Labor and Employment as represented by Director ATTY. ERWIN N. AQUINO, Regional Office No. IV-A with office address at 3rd & 4th Floors, Andenson Bldg. II, Brgy. Parian, Calamba City, Laguna.

and

NAME OF INTERN POSTAL/HOME ADDRESS EFFECTIVE START DATE EFFECTIVE END DATE OFFICE AND PLACE OF ASSIGNMENT WORK OR DETAILS OF ASSIGNMENT WORK CONTACT PERSON

WITNESSETH:

  1. This Agreement governs the Intern’s engagement with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) or the government agency, which aims to provide valuable opportunity for interns to gain practical experience, develop skills, and contribute to public service. The Program does not establish an employer-employee relationship and services rendered will not be credited as government service.
  2. This Agreement governs the Intern’s engagement with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) or the government agency, which aims to provide valuable opportunity for interns to gain practical experience, develop skills, and contribute to public service. The Program does not establish an employer-employee relationship and services rendered will not be credited as government service.
  1. The internship period shall cover a minimum of 3 months and a maximum of 6 months, starting from the date the intern reports to their assigned office. For computation, one (1) month shall be equivalent to twenty-two (22) working days, excluding special non-working holidays. The duration shall be for a minimum of three (3) months and a maximum of six (6) months, or for a minimum of six (6) months and a maximum of twelve (12) months, as applicable. Interns may be considered for extension, subject to applicable rules, upon the recommendation of the partner agency after completing their initial engagement. However, the combined duration of the internship shall not exceed twelve (12) months. Implementation may extend to the immediately succeeding calendar year, subject to availability of funds and applicable rules.
  2. Each beneficiary shall receive a stipend equivalent to one hundred percent (100%) of the highest minimum prevailing wage in the region;
  3. The interns shall be entitled to accident insurance effective at least one day prior to internship's commencement. The DOLE shall cover the premium from its administrative cost and no deductions shall be made from the intern's stipend;
  4. This Agreement may be terminated early by the DOLE, motu proprio or upon recommendation of the partner agency, and subject to due process, for any of the following grounds:
    1. Habitual absenteeism;
    2. Willful disobedience to the agency's rules or persistent insubordination towards lawful orders from the supervisor;
    3. Theft or intentional damage of the agency's property;
    4. Engaging in violence and/or misconduct during the internship or on the agency's premises by the intern; and
    5. Consistent poor performance over an extended period, despite receiving appropriate guidance.
  5. GIP beneficiaries may be considered for extension or re-engagement, subject to the availability of funds, under the following conditions:
    1. They are recommended by their immediate supervisor or partner agency, with a satisfactory performance rating or evaluation;
    2. They are assigned to tasks that support further skills development or the acquisition of additional competencies;
    3. The total internship period shall not exceed twelve (12) months;
  6. Compensatory time off (CTO) policies do not apply to the GIP. Overtime services accumulate to eight (8) hours shall be computed as one (1) working day. This also applies to services rendered on weekends, non-working holidays, and other overtime work, all of which will be included in the total duration of engagement.
  7. Interns who wish to end the internship early must notify the DOLE at least five (5) days in advance. DOLE may assign a replacement intern for either the

remaining period of the original term or only for the unexpired portion of the agreement.

If the Intern is unable to complete the internship program, they must inform their immediate supervisor at the GIP Partner Agency, and report to the DOLE-Regional Office about their intention to end the Agreement early.

  1. Interns are expected to perform assigned tasks and activities, designed to enhance their experience, knowledge, skills, and competencies in line with the objectives of the GIP, aligned and consistent with their capacity for growth and the approved Work Plan forming part of this Agreement:
    1. Clerical or secretarial work, such as encoding, typing, filing, sorting, and photocopying;
    2. Customer service functions, such as answering telephone calls and attending to official concerns of visitors, and profiling;
    3. Research activities, such as data gathering, survey administration, and other related undertakings;
    4. Staff support functions, such as providing logistical assistance and other forms of support in staff work.

Field Assignments provided to interns, related to environmental protection, climate change mitigation, or other activities that contribute to poverty reduction, are strongly encouraged. Field assignments should be limited to the intern's city, municipality, or locality.

Interns/beneficiaries shall not perform any task or assignment involving activities related to political patronage, sabotage or any election-related activities during the election period, which are strictly prohibited, nor shall they be assigned to tasks or assignments which are hazardous and potentially risky in nature and are non-compliant with all general labor standards and occupational safety and health standards as defined by Republic Act No. 11058 and its Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations.

  1. Interns must register with the PEIS/PhilJobNet to monitor their employment status (whether absorbed, hired by other government agency, and employment with private sector), immediately after the internship period to access employment opportunities;
  2. Interns shall accomplish the GIP Feedback Form immediately after the engagement;
  3. Interns are expected to maintain strict confidentiality regarding any non-public information acquired during their internship. This obligation applies both throughout the duration of the internship and continues indefinitely after its conclusion, both during and after the engagement.
  4. Interns shall comply with the orientation, training/seminar, documentary, reportorial, and all other requirements pertinent to the fulfillment of this Agreement.

By signing this Agreement, the interns agree to the following supplementary provisions:

  1. Ensure completeness and truthfulness of submitted information in the GIP application form;
  2. Provide services in a professional manner with honor and integrity; and
  3. Provide an update to DOLE on the employment status thirty (30) days after completion of the program, before issuing any certifications. Additionally, provide another update ninety (90) days later. This is part of the program monitoring to ensure the effectiveness of the program implementation and to identify any future interventions and support needed for the GIP beneficiaries, in accordance with the monitoring and reporting requirements under the applicable GIP Guidelines.

Confidentiality: The Intern must maintain confidentiality, when and where appropriate, during and after the internship, of all the data and information where such information is not already within the public domain and is indicated or understood to be confidential.
